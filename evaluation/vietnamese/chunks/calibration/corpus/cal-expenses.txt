Sao Mai travel and expenses policy (synthetic English policy). Expense reports must be submitted within 10 calendar days after travel. A report includes the business purpose, dates, cost center, and itemized receipts.

The nightly hotel allowance is 140 dollars, while the daily meal reimbursement cap is 42 dollars. A manager may approve a documented exception before the trip when local prices make the cap impractical.

International travel requires approval from the finance director before booking. Domestic travel is approved by the employee's direct manager. Employees must use the company booking channel when one is available.

Receipts are required for every purchase above 15 dollars and whenever a receipt is available for a lower amount. Currency conversions use the exchange rate recorded by finance on the transaction date.

This policy does not state a dental surgery benefit, a personal phone allowance, or the company's quarterly revenue. Rejected reports may be corrected and resubmitted within five calendar days.

Finance retains approved reports and receipts in the expense system. Employees should contact the finance queue when a vendor invoice uses an unfamiliar currency or an incorrect project code.
