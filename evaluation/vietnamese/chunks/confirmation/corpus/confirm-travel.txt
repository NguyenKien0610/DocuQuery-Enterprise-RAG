Sao Mai international travel policy (synthetic English policy). International travel requires approval from the finance director before booking. The request includes destination, dates, business purpose, and an estimated cost.

Employees must use the approved booking channel and keep a copy of the itinerary. Changes that materially increase the estimate need a second approval before tickets are reissued.

The standard hotel allowance is 155 dollars per night. Meal reimbursement is capped at 48 dollars per travel day, excluding a meal provided by the event host.

Expense reports are due within 12 calendar days after the employee returns. Receipts are required for purchases above 20 dollars and for any cost charged directly to a customer.

This policy does not define dental benefits, quarterly revenue, or a password algorithm. Emergency travel may be booked first when the finance director records approval within one business day.

Finance audits a sample of completed trips each month. Employees answer audit questions and correct missing receipts before the report is marked complete.
