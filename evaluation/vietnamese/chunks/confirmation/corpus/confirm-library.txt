Sao Mai library maintenance policy (synthetic English policy). The library closes for maintenance on Wednesday afternoon from 13:00 to 17:00. A notice is posted at least three days before an exceptional closure.

Readers may return borrowed books through the lobby drop box while the service desk is closed. Staff empty the box twice on the next open day and record any damaged item.

Reference books remain available for on-site reading during normal hours. Loan renewals are allowed once when another reader has not placed a hold.

The library manager reviews the collection each quarter and removes obsolete copies after a documented decision. New access cards are issued at the service desk.

This policy does not specify a daily late fee, a visitor's personal phone number, or the company's vehicle-loan interest rate. Those facts are outside its scope.

Maintenance work orders identify the affected room, responsible technician, and completion time. The manager archives the notice after the normal schedule resumes.
