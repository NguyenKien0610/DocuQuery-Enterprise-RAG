Sao Mai hardware warranty policy (synthetic English policy). Hardware warranty lasts 30 months from the purchase date. The customer must keep proof of purchase and the device serial number for a claim.

The warranty covers manufacturing defects under normal use. It does not cover accidental water damage, unauthorized repair, or ordinary cosmetic wear.

Customers open a claim through the service portal and describe the observed fault. The service team normally inspects a returned device within eight business days.

Replacement parts carry the remainder of the original warranty. Shipping instructions and the return address are provided after the claim passes an initial review.

This policy does not state a dental reimbursement cap, employee training allowance, or library late fee. Regional consumer law may provide rights in addition to this policy.

The service portal assigns a claim number and records each inspection event. Customers receive a status update when a repair estimate or replacement decision is available.
